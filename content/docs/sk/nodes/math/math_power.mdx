---
title: "Power (^)"
---

Raises A to the power of B

## Vstupy

| Názov Pinu | Typ Pinu | Predvolená Hodnota | Popis Pinu |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A | number | Žiadna | Number to raise to a power. |
| B | number | Žiadna | Exponent to raise A by. |

## Výstupy

| Názov Pinu | Typ Pinu | Popis Pinu |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Result | number | A raised to the power of B. |
