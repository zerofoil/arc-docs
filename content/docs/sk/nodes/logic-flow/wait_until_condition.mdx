---
title: "Wait Until Condition"
---

Checks a boolean condition until it becomes true. Times out through the Timed Out branch, polling is bounded.

## Vstupy

| Názov Pinu | Typ Pinu | Predvolená Hodnota | Popis Pinu |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Exec | exec |  | Starts checking the condition. |
| Condition | boolean | false | Waits until this value becomes true. |
| Timeout (ms) | number | 30000 | Maximum time to wait before taking the Timed Out branch, in milliseconds. |
| Check Every (ms) | number | 250 | Time between condition checks, in milliseconds. |

## Výstupy

| Názov Pinu | Typ Pinu | Popis Pinu |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Ready | exec | Runs when the condition becomes true. |
| Timed Out | exec | Runs when the condition does not become true before the timeout. |
